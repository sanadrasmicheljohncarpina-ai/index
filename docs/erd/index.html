Cross-diagram foreign keys

These declared relationships connect tables shown in different diagrams.

  • People and access → Evaluation records: evaluation_tracker.evaluator_id and target_user_id; evaluation_submissions.evaluator_id and target_user_id; evaluation_results.target_user_id.
  • People and access → Questionnaires: questionnaire_forms.created_by.
  • People and access → Administration and operations: analytics_reports.generated_by and documents.uploaded_by.
  • Evaluation records → Questionnaires: evaluation_tracker.form_id, evaluation_submissions.form_id, evaluation_results.form_id and question_id, and questionnaire_answers.user_question_id.
  • Evaluation records → People and access: evaluation_results.target_user_id, plus evaluator and target references listed above.

Diagram connectors show only foreign keys declared by MySQL. Application-level references without a declared constraint, such as some reminder and certification IDs, are not drawn as enforced relationships.
