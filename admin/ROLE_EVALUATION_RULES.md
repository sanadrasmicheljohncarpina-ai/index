# Role-Based Evaluation Rules

## 1. Teacher / Teaching Staff
A person with a Teacher function is visible to students only where they are assigned to teach.

- Junior High / Senior High: must match the student's assigned year level.
- College: must match the student's assignment and the active evaluation semester.
- A teaching assignment does not automatically create a Multi-Role evaluation.

## 2. Non-Teaching Staff
A Staff member with no teaching/year-level assignment is institution-wide.

- Appears on the Student Dashboard Staff context across all year levels.
- Uses that person's Staff question set.
- If the same person also has a genuine additional responsibility, they also receive a separate Multi-Role context with different questions.

## 3. Multi-Role
Multi-Role is created only by a real additional responsibility.

Examples include an explicit secondary role or an additional designation/responsibility such as Coordinator, Department Head, Program Head, Adviser, Officer, Executive Assistant, etc.

- Multi-Role visibility is institution-wide across year levels.
- It is a separate evaluation context, not a replacement for the base role.
- A non-teaching Staff member with another responsibility may appear in both Staff and Multi-Role.
- A Teacher or teaching Staff member without a matching Grade/Year assignment does not appear in that base teaching context, but can still appear in Multi-Role when a genuine additional responsibility exists.
- Teaching assignments alone never create Multi-Role.

## 4. EA Evaluation
The Questionnaire's former School Head evaluation mode is presented as **EA Evaluation**.

The Executive Assistant can assign a separate per-person question set for:

- Principal
- Dean
- Non-teaching Staff

These EA forms are separate from Student Evaluation and Peer-to-Peer question sets.

## 5. Question Separation
The same person may legitimately have more than one evaluation context.

For example, a non-teaching Staff member who is also a Coordinator can have:

1. Staff questions — evaluated as Staff.
2. Multi-Role questions — evaluated for the additional Coordinator responsibility.

The submissions are tracked as separate evaluation contexts.
